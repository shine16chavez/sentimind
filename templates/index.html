SentiMind

Why SentiMind?

A Web-Based Mental Health Prediction and Sentiment Analysis System.

The Problem

The Silent Crisis

Mental health struggles often go unspoken in physical spaces but leave deep traces in digital conversations. Traditional monitoring relies on manual reporting, often missing subtle linguistic cues of Anxiety, Bipolar, or Depression until it's too late.

Early Intervention

The Critical Window

Identifying Depression, Bipolar, Anxiety and Suicidal Ideation at the earliest stages is the difference between a recovery plan and a medical emergency. SentiMind analyzes text to find the "tipping point" where a Normal state begins to shift into clinical distress.

The Innovation

Ensemble Intelligence & Bagging

SentiMind utilizes a dual-engine architecture. We employ Bagging (Random Forest) to categorize text into six specific mental health states with high stability. Simultaneously, our sentiment analysis runs through a Manual Ensemble of VADER and TextBlob, combining lexicon-based intensity with polarity and subjectivity metrics to ensure robust triage.

Our Mission

SentiMind's Goal

Our mission is to empower communities with a proactive tool that predicts Anxiety, Depression, Bipolar, or Suicidal ideation through advanced NLP. By mapping complex clinical signatures to a clear Negative/Neutral scale, we provide a triage system that respects privacy while flagging those who need support most.

Our Technical Process

01
Initial Cleaning

Rigorous data sanitation: dropped nulls, stripped redundant columns, and applied regex `[^a-zA-Z\s]` for linguistic normalization.

02
IG and Entropy Analysis

Measured Shannon Entropy ($H$) to quantify dataset impurity and calculated Information Gain for every token.

03
Feature Denoising

Filtered vocabulary using a $0.001$ IG threshold to remove statistical noise and focus on clinical markers.

04
Random Forest Bagging

Deployed a Bagging ensemble of 100 Decision Trees to classify status across 6 mental health categories.

05
Manual Sentiment Ensemble

Combined VADER intensity with TextBlob polarity for broad Negative/Neutral triage.

06
Flask Integration

Real-time inference via serialized Pickle models and asynchronous API requests.
